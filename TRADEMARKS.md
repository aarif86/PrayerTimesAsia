# Using the Prayer Times Asia name

The code is open for everyone ([NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md)). The name, the app icon and
the prayertimes.asia address are not. They tell people "this is the free, ad-free,
private app they trust", so they stay with the official project.

## Welcome, no need to ask

- Saying your app is "based on Prayer Times Asia" or "forked from Prayer Times Asia".
- Writing about, reviewing or recommending the app, with screenshots.
- Masjids, schools and authorities linking to the app or the website, and showing
  the official masjid screen on their TV.
- Running the code for your own masjid or community under your own name.

## Please ask first

Email salam@prayertimes.asia before:

- using the name or icon on printed material, merchandise or an event;
- describing your organisation as a partner or sponsor;
- publishing a version for an authority or masjid network that mentions us.

## Not allowed

- Publishing a copy in any app store, or as a website, under the name
  "Prayer Times Asia" or a confusingly similar name or icon.
- Suggesting that a copy is the official app, or that we endorse it.
- Using the name or icon in a version that shows ads, sells data or tracks people.

If you see a copy pretending to be this app, please tell us at salam@prayertimes.asia.
