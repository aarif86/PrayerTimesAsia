# Changelog

What's new in Prayer Times Asia, newest first.

## Day and night, everywhere

*1.0.0 (build 9) · 25 October 2026*

Automatic now follows the sky, a light/dark switch on the Prayer screen, a fifth style, and the big widget in every style.

**New**

- Centre Dial: the Day Dial in the middle, with the day and the list below.
- Light/dark switch beside the style button: Automatic, Light or Dark.
- The big widget comes in every style: Classic list, Day Dial, Sun Path or Colour Bands, or the same as the app.

**Improved**

- Automatic follows the sky: bright from sunrise to Maghrib, a soft dim night after.
- Every tab shares the same live sky (no more sun on Falak at night).

## Your look, one tap away

*1.0.0 (build 8) · 24 October 2026*

Switch Prayer screen styles from the Prayer screen, a simpler set of tabs, and travel features when you need them.

**New**

- Palette button on the Prayer screen: tap for the next style, touch and hold to pick one. Your choice is kept.

**Improved**

- Settings is now a main tab: Prayer, Qibla, Falak, Masjid, Settings.
- Musafir features are optional. When you land in another country we ask if you'd like jamak and qasar guidance; Journey opens from Settings or the plane chip.
- Dhuha and the blessed night times are in English for everyone.
- Masjid: an (i) button tells you who built the app, instead of a card at the end of the list.

## Four ways to see your day

*1.0.0 (build 7) · 24 October 2026*

Choose how the Prayer screen looks, and meet the people behind the app.

**New**

- Prayer screen styles in Settings > Display: Classic list (default), Day Dial, Sun Path and Colour Bands.
- Day Dial: the whole day as a 24-hour ring in the colours of the sky, with every prayer time inside it.
- Colour Bands: each prayer a full-width band of colour, a tribute to the classic Singapore timetable.
- Sun Path: the sun's arc across today's sky, with each prayer marked on it.
- From the makers: Badal Haji & Umrah (Badal.sg) and NasarDigital, shown only under Show more and in Settings. Turn them off any time.

**Improved**

- Classic list fills the screen: everything, including Show more and the official MUIS source, without scrolling.
- The Day Dial follows the natural colours of the day: bright dawn for Subuh, hot noon for Zohor, sunset for Maghrib, night for Isyak.

## One calm colour palette

*1.0.0 (build 6) · 23 October 2026*

The same colours everywhere, and a simpler Qibla compass.

**Improved**

- One colour palette across the whole app: brand peach, qibla gold, prayer colours only on prayer rows.
- Qibla: the centre is a single arrow pointing at the qibla, instead of moving numbers.

A watch with the Ka'bah at its heart.

**New**

- New app icon: a compass-bezel watch with the Ka'bah at the centre and skeleton sword hands at 8:02.

## Times first

*1.0.0 (build 4) · 22 October 2026*

A redesign around what matters most: the prayer times.

**New**

- Show more / Show less with Dhuha, the middle and the last third of the night (and Imsak in Ramadan). Free.
- Light mode with a gently breathing sun and drifting clouds; Automatic, Light or Dark.
- Falak is its own tab, with an AR camera view and a spirit level.
- Prayer alerts at the exact time, with an optional early reminder and a Syuruk alert.
- Qibla turns green when you face it, with a tick, a ripple and a tap, plus step-by-step instructions.
- Prayer names follow you: Subuh or Fajr from your home country or phone, with the local word shown when you travel.
- Big Home Screen widget: today's timetable, a mini version of the app.

**Improved**

- Large, clear times with AM/PM always on the same line; the countdown is small at the top.
- New consistent icon set: prayer mat, compass, telescope, masjid and plane.

## Falak Mode

*1.0.0 (build 3) · 22 October 2026*

Find the qibla and Asr from the sun, the classical way.

**New**

- Falak Mode: Qibla Day, rashdul qiblat, shadow-stick Asr and sky signs, clearly labelled and pending review.
- Ramadan and Eid dates with their sources, and 'My community follows' setting.
- Subscribe to official prayer times in your calendar (MUIS and every JAKIM zone).
- Help verify times where you are: send a masjid timetable photo, no account needed.
- Travellers see today's combined (jamak) prayer windows.

## First build

*1.0.0 (build 2) · 22 October 2026*

Accurate, offline prayer times for Muslims living and travelling across Asia.

**New**

- Prayer times for every country in Asia, calculated on the phone and working offline.
- Official timetables when online: MUIS (Singapore), JAKIM (Malaysia, by zone) and Kemenag (Indonesia).
- Checked against a full year of official tables: offline times are never earlier than official.
- Every day shows its source: official, masjid, community or calculated.
- Travel mode: switches country method automatically, with musafir guidance and a trip log.
- Qibla compass, nearby masjid and musollah search, Home and Lock Screen widgets, Live Activity countdown.
- Optional prayer tracker with streak, and a Subuh wake-up alarm.
- No third-party ads, no accounts, no tracking. Your location never leaves your phone.
