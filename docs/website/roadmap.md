# Roadmap

Where Prayer Times Asia is heading.

## Now

- **App Store and Google Play**. The iPhone and Android apps leave testing and go free on the App Store and Google Play. Phones come first, and every other device joins when it is ready.
- **The whole app in Malay and Indonesian**. Every screen in Bahasa Melayu and Bahasa Indonesia, not only the prayer names and Ramadan.
- **One place for alerts and alarms**. Set each prayer to Off, Notify or Alarm in one screen, with a check that tells you what might stop it ringing.
- **Falak review**. A member of the falak committee in Singapore checks Falak Mode before it loses its 'pending review' label.

## Next

- **Mac**. The same app on your Mac, with the next prayer in the menu bar and alerts. A Mac has no compass, so the qibla compass stays on the phone.
- **Apple Watch and Wear OS**. The next prayer on your wrist, a gentle tap when it is time, and the qibla inside the watch app.
- **Windows**. The web app as a Windows app from the Microsoft Store, with the next prayer beside the clock and alerts.
- **TV at home**. Today's times on the big screen on Apple TV, Android TV and Google TV.
- **Time Sensitive alerts on iPhone**. Prayer notifications that come through a Work or Do Not Disturb Focus, like the alarms already do.
- **Masjid screen with local masjids**. Trying the free masjid display screen with a few masjids in Singapore first.

## Later

- **Badal Haji season**. Order reminders around six months before Aidiladha for Badal Haji.
- **Badal.my for Malaysia**. Badal Haji and Umrah for Malaysians, shown to users in Malaysia.
- **Masjid timetables**. Jamaah times and Jumaat from local masjids, checked by the community.
