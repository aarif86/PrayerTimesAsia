# Roadmap

Where Prayer Times Asia is heading.

## Now

- **Falak review** - A member of the falak committee in Singapore checks Falak Mode before it loses its 'pending review' label.

## Next

- **Time Sensitive prayer alerts** - Alerts that break through Focus modes so you never miss a prayer.
- **Badal Haji season** - Order reminders around six months before Aidiladha for Badal Haji.
- **Badal.my for Malaysia** - Badal Haji & Umrah for Malaysians, shown to users in Malaysia.

## Later

- **App Store launch** - From TestFlight to the App Store, free.
- **Masjid timetables** - Jamaah times and Jumu'ah from local masjids, verified by the community.
- **Website** - Changelog, roadmap and feature requests on the web.
