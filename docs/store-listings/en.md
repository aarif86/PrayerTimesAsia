# Store listing, English

Copy and paste each box as it is. Every box has its limit in the heading, and
`python3 tools/check_store_copy.py` checks the lengths and the house style
(no colons, no dashes) before anything goes to a store.

Used for the App Store (English), Google Play (English, default listing) and
the Samsung Galaxy Store.

## App Store

### Description (limit 4000)

```text
Know when to pray and which way to face, at home or wherever you land. Asia first, and now in 143 countries.

Ustaz Aarif Raziff once landed in Vietnam and could not find a proper prayer app. So he came home and built one that does one thing well. Prayer.

TIMES YOU CAN TRUST
• Singapore follows the official MUIS timetable
• Malaysia follows the official JAKIM e-Solat timetable for your zone
• Indonesia follows Kemenag, the Ministry of Religious Affairs
• Saudi Arabia, the Gulf, Türkiye and others follow their own religious authority
• Everywhere else uses the method your region knows, checked against official times
• Kept on your phone, so it works without internet

TRAVEL MADE EASY
• Your times switch by themselves the moment you land
• A short welcome card tells you what changed
• Musafir help shows how far you are from home and when you may shorten (qasar) and combine (jamak) your prayers, for Shafi'i and Hanafi

QIBLA
• A calm compass that turns green when you face the Kaaba
• Check it with the sun on Qibla Day, or with a simple shadow stick

NEVER MISS SUBUH
• An alert for each prayer, and a reminder before it begins
• Wake up alarms for Subuh and tahajjud, with snooze
• A gentle reminder on Friday for Jumaat

YOUR MASJID
• Find a masjid or musollah near you
• Pick your own masjid to see its jamaah times and a countdown to iqamah

RAMADAN AND SYAWAL
• Countdown to Imsak and Berbuka
• Sahur alarm, and a Tarawih counter for 8 or 20 rakaat
• Your fasting record, with the days still to replace
• The six days of Syawal

MADE FOR YOU
• Five looks for the Prayer screen, with a sky that follows the real sun
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and a live countdown in the Dynamic Island
• Big and Simple mode with large, clear text
• Prayer names in English, Malay, Arabic, Urdu, Bangla or French
• A short hadith now and then to lift the heart, which you can turn off
• A prayer tracker to help you keep your five each day
• Move your settings to a new phone with a QR code

PRIVATE AND FREE
• No sign up and no account
• No outside ads and no tracking
• Your exact location never leaves your phone
• Free, with nothing to buy, and open source

Times are worked out with care and checked against official timetables. Your local masjid may differ by a minute or two. Always follow your local religious authority.

Built fisabilillah in Singapore by Ustaz Aarif Raziff and Nasar Digital (Nasar Pte Ltd).
```
