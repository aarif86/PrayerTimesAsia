# Journey Prayer Planner, for the ustaz to check

Page: prayertimes.asia/journey-planner/ (Malay /ms/perancang-perjalanan/, Indonesian /id/perencana-perjalanan/). Free, runs in the browser, nothing is sent anywhere.

## What it does (facts only)
Enter cities and local takeoff and landing times for up to four connected flights. It shows which prayer time is in force before takeoff, in the air, during a stopover and after landing, with the time each prayer begins and ends, and the Qibla bearing at the start of any time in the air.

Times use the same engine and the same method per country as the app. In the air the plane is placed on a straight path between the two cities and the method of the nearer city is used. The page says this is an estimate.

## The one travel prayer paragraph (needs your check)
English: "A traveller may have different options for how to pray, and scholars explain the details differently, including how to treat prayer times in the air. This planner only shows the times. Please ask your own ustaz and follow your local religious authority."

Malay and Indonesian are drafts of the same sentence (web/journey/words.ts, keys travelH and travelP). Nothing says shorten or join. Nothing is said about which place's time applies in the air.
