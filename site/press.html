The story

When Ustaz Aarif Raziff landed in Vietnam, he couldn't find a proper prayer app. The big ones showed ads around salah or asked for a subscription, and even the best one he found wasn't made for Asia.

Back home in Singapore, he decided to build one. Prayer Times Asia is fully focused on salah. It follows the local religious authority in each country, uses official timetables where they exist, works offline and keeps your exact location on your phone.

It is built fisabilillah, in the hope that it becomes sadaqah jariah. His company, Nasar Digital (Nasar Pte Ltd), pays the running costs, and the app will stay free, with no ads and its code open for anyone to check.
