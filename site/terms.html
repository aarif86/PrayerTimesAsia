Terms of use

Updated 22 November 2026. These terms cover the website, the web app, the masjid screen, and the iPhone and Android apps.

The short version

Prayer Times Asia is free for everyone. Use it to help you pray on time, and always follow your local religious authority and your masjid when they differ from the app.

Who makes it

Prayer Times Asia is built fisabilillah in Singapore by Ustaz Aarif Raziff and Ustazah Nasibah Hamzah, with Nasar Digital (Nasar Pte Ltd).

Prayer times are a guide

Where we can, the app uses the official timetable from the local religious authority. Everywhere else it works the times out on your device with the method used in that country. We check the times carefully, but a calculation, a download or your device clock can sometimes be wrong. If something looks off, check with your masjid and tell us.

Alerts and alarms

Your phone decides when alerts and alarms can ring. Silent mode, Focus, battery saving and other phone settings can stop them. Please do not rely on the app alone for something you must not miss.

Qibla and falak

The Qibla compass depends on your phone's sensors, which can be thrown off by metal and magnets. The falak tools are for learning and checking. Never look straight at the sun.

Free, with no paid version

There is nothing to buy in the app. Barakah Picks shows a few halal Muslim businesses and causes that we check by hand, and you can turn it off in Settings.

Your privacy

What the app keeps and sends is explained on our privacy page.
