Privacy

Updated 17 November 2026. This covers the website, the web app, the masjid screen, and the iPhone and Android apps.

The short version

No outside ads, no accounts, and no tracking across other apps or sites. Your exact location stays on your device. To understand how many people use Prayer Times Asia and where, the apps send a few anonymous counts, and you can turn them off.

Barakah Picks

Under Show more on the Prayer screen there may be one small Barakah Picks card, for our own services or a halal Muslim business or cause we checked by hand (paid ones are marked Sponsored). It is a plain link built into the app, with no code or tracker from anyone else. If anonymous counts are on, the app also counts, once a day, that a card was shown, and counts each tap. That count carries the card's name, where in the app it was, and the same anonymous details as the counts below, never who you are. We share only totals with the business, such as how many times their card was seen and tapped in each country. If you tap, the website opens with a tag saying the visit came from Prayer Times Asia, and nothing about you. You can turn the cards off in Settings, and turning off anonymous counts stops these counts too.

What stays on your device

Your location, saved places, settings, alerts and prayer tracker are kept on your phone or in your browser. Prayer times are worked out on the device, or taken from the official timetable it downloads (the request names only the year and the zone, never your exact spot).

Anonymous usage counts

When the app opens (at most once an hour), once a day with a short total for the day before, and when a masjid screen is running (a few times a day), it sends a short note to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-first analytics service based in the EU. The note contains:

SentWhy
A random number made on your deviceTo count people, not visits. It is not linked to your name, phone number, email or Apple or Google account, and TelemetryDeck scrambles it again before storing it.
The city and country of the place you use for prayer timesTo know where to add official timetables. Never your coordinates or street.
App version, iPhone, Android or web, system version, languageTo know what to support and test.
Masjid screens only: the masjid's name and the screen's lookTo know which masjids use the screen, so we can help if something goes wrong.
Once a day, for the day before: how many minutes the app was on screen, how many times it was opened, and which parts were used (Prayer, Qibla, Falak, Masjid, Settings)To know how the app helps people day to day, and which parts to improve. It is one short note a day with totals only, never what you did at what time.
For Barakah Picks, which card was shown or tapped, and where in the appTo tell the businesses and causes on Barakah Picks how many people saw and tapped their card, as totals only.

TelemetryDeck does not keep IP addresses and sets no cookies. On this website it counts page visits the same way (a visitor's number changes every day).

Turning the counts off

  • iPhone, Android and web app: Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage counts.
  • Masjid screen: on the setup page, untick "Let Prayer Times Asia know this screen is running", then send the new link to the TV.
  • Browsers with Global Privacy Control are never counted.

Things you choose to send

If you use "Help verify times", the masjid timetable photo and what you type are sent so we can check them. If you email us, we see your email. If you join the Timekeepers, the details you type in the form are kept in our contact system so we can write to you about Timekeepers work. Email us at any time to have them removed. Nearby masjid search on iPhone goes through Apple Maps; on Android and the web it sends only the map area to OpenStreetMap services.
