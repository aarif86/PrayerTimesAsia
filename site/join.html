Prayer Times Asia Timekeepers

Become a Timekeeper.

For centuries the great masjids had a muwaqqit, a timekeeper who watched the sky and told the people when to pray. Today we share that work. Timekeepers are volunteers who help keep the prayer times right for their own city.

Join the Timekeepers What will I do?

Free to join. A few minutes a month. Help in your own time.

What Timekeepers do

Small help that reaches many.

Pick what suits you. Nobody has to do everything.

🕒

Check the times for your city

Compare the app with the official timetable where you live and tell us if a minute is off.

📱

Test on your device

Phone, tablet, watch, computer or TV. Try new versions early and tell us what breaks.

🕌

Bring your masjid on board

Help your masjid show the prayer times on its screen and keep its details correct.

🌍

Read it in your language

Check that the words in the app sound right and proper in the language you know best.

🌙

The check before each new month

Before each Hijri month, and before Ramadan most of all, Timekeepers look over the times together.

🤝

Tell a friend

Share the app with your family, your class or your masjid. One good word goes a long way.
