Prayer Times Asia is a free prayer app, built fisabilillah in Singapore by Ustaz Aarif Raziff and Nasar Digital (Nasar Pte Ltd).

It shows the times of your local religious authority first, in 143 countries. MUIS in Singapore, JAKIM in Malaysia and Kemenag in Indonesia are shown exactly as they are. You also get alarms for every prayer, reminders, the qibla, masjids near you, Ramadan and Syawal, and a Hijri calendar. It works offline.

No outside ads, no subscriptions, no sign up, and your exact location stays on your phone.

This is a test version before the App Store. Things change from build to build, and now and then something may break. Please tell us when it does.

Always follow your local religious authority.
